# Coast TT project rules

- **Event admission.** Apply [REQ-008](spec.md#req-008) when changing event registration. Open admission accepts eligible self-entries automatically while capacity remains and waitlists overflow. Closed admission sends self-entries to competition staff for approval. Apply the registration window and capacity checks in the API. Acceptance grants a place and payment is tracked separately.
- **Verification.** When changing admission, cover both modes, a full open event, and the authorized organizer's decision in [EVAL-008](test.md#eval-008).
- **Geography and authority.** Use country, region, and city in signup, club setup, and geographic rankings. Seed the country catalogue and Kenya's counties and towns. Enforce roles in the API. The platform super admin has global authority. Regional and country officials oversee their assigned jurisdictions: they read its clubs as members do and approve publication, but hold no club permission by appointment alone (user decision, 2026-11-19); a regional body runs the region's agenda, articles, meetings and sponsorships (REQ-048). Clubs run their own tournaments. Regional officials approve regional publication, country officials approve national publication, and platform super admins approve international publication. See [the geographic authority tasks](tasks.md#current-feature-checklist).
- **Back office.** `/admin` is the app's own back office for platform super admins ([REQ-056](spec.md#req-056)), never Django admin. Its API routes sit under `/api/v1/platform/`, answer "not found" to everyone else, and need an entry in the privacy matrix. Django admin is a fallback at `/django-admin/` that the proxy does not serve.
- **Fees and check-in.** Let organizers choose free or paid and require a registration fee for paid tournaments. On the tournament's local dates, accepted players can check themselves in. Keep the official desk check-in available for players without the app. See [the tournament workflow tasks](tasks.md#current-feature-checklist).
- **Draw and schedule.** Recommend tables automatically and let staff edit assignments. Recommend seeds by rating points, then recommend a schedule from the draw. Let organizers review and edit each recommendation before submitting it. See [the draw and scheduling tasks](tasks.md#current-feature-checklist).
- **Jumbles.** Support a mixed-doubles round robin with equal-sized men's and women's player pools, a different partner in each round, and final standings based on each player's cumulative points. See [the Jumbles task](tasks.md#current-feature-checklist).
- **Facilities.** Facilities register as facility organizations; each branch is a venue with its own location, hours, tables, rules and benefits. Staff set day and longer passes, record door payments (cash, M-Pesa reference) without processing payments, and publish training schedules and events. Players see only their own visits. See [REQ-044](spec.md#req-044) and [the facility tasks](tasks.md#current-feature-checklist).
- **Governance and meetings.** Each club has exactly one nominated club admin with full club authority; others act only through delegated roles. Clubs, regional bodies and the national body have committees with configurable positions and fields. Clubs nominate representatives to their regional body, and regions to the national body. Any level holds meetings with agendas, check-in attendance, and optional minutes (decisions, action items, next date), and hosts non-competition events for invited reps or everyone. See [REQ-045](spec.md#req-045) and [REQ-046](spec.md#req-046).
