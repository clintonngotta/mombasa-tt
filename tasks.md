## Execution rules

Complete documents → create/review Stitch screens → application coding. Work through dependencies; later-phase readiness does not authorize starting a production broadcast or charging a subscription. All application tasks below begin unchecked. For a completed task append evidence (test report, reviewed artifact or release), date, and commit where available. A partial task stays unchecked. An EVAL reference means its applicable cases must pass, not that a test already exists.

Phase dependencies: Foundation → 1 → 2 → 3. Phase 4 depends on Phase 1 and its media policy gate; Phase 5 depends on Phase 4; Phase 6 depends on approved commercial policy and production operational readiness. The order shown is the delivery baseline; changing it requires updating intent.md.

- [x] **TASK-022** — Implement team rosters, tie templates, lineup submission and sequential rubbers. Depends: TASK-019. REQ-011 / EVAL-011. Done: invalid lineups fail and clinched ties leave surplus rubbers unplayed. Done 2026-11-18: a team event carries its tie format (`tie_rubbers`: up to 9 singles or doubles in playing order, and `tie_wins_needed`, at most half of them rounded up so a tie always has a winner; four singles around a doubles, first to 3, when not given), frozen with the rest of the structure when the event starts. Each match of a team draw is a tie. `competitions/ties.py`: a team's captain or competition staff hand in its lineup (`POST .../matches/{id}/lineup/`): one roster player per singles, two different ones per doubles, a player may play several rubbers; refused when a rubber is missing, the wrong size, or names somebody off the roster. Rubbers are decided strictly in order, one at a time; at the wins needed the tie is decided, the rest become `not_played` with no score, and the winner moves through the draw like any other match.
